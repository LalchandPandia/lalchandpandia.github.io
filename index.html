About

I am a fifth-year PhD student in computer science at the University of Chicago, advised by Dr. Allyson Ettinger. I study how LLMs can learn over time and how LLM-based agents can work together.

My research spans data selection, continual learning, and multi-agent communication and evaluation. My work examines whether training-data coverage matters more than data difficulty, how to improve a target capability while limiting unintended changes to model behavior, and how access to information affects coordination among agents. I also work closely with Dr. Claire Donnat and Dr. Kanishka Misra.

Before my PhD, I worked in industry on distributed file systems and data replication. I did my M.Tech. in Computer Science at IIT Kanpur.

I'm looking for industry research roles and am open to collaborations. Feel free to reach out at lcpandia@uchicago.edu.
